Responsible research

This is a mind map of the Research Ethics for Doctoral Students course. The map is organised as a tree, but in reality things are more interwined (something about "research security" can also fall under "legal compliance"). The numbers in parenthesis correspond to a section of the ethics course MOOC. What is not covered by the ethics course can be provided as external links. Missing bits are being added to a new version of the course. The links right now are just placeholders and real links are still missing.
